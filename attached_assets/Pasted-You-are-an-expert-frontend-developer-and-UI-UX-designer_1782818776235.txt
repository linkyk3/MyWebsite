You are an expert frontend developer and UI/UX designer specializing in high-end brutalist, early web, and Y2K aesthetic revivals. Build a fully functional, highly interactive, modern portfolio website for a student double-majoring in Urban Planning and Industrial Engineering.

### 1. CONCEPT & AESTHETIC PRINCIPLES
- **Core Aesthetic:** A "Neo-Y2K Flash Revival." It must capture the structural frames, experimental layouts, and desktop-interface nostalgia of early 2000s Flash websites (inspired by classic sites like thinkmad-2002 and tom-nulens-2002), but execute it with sleek, ultra-modern CSS capabilities like backdrop-filters and high-end blur layers.
- **Color Palette:** High-contrast Monochrome. Pure white, stark blacks, deep grays, and EXACTLY ONE vivid accent color: Electric Red (`#FF0000`). Use the red sparingly for hover states, active menu markers, typing carets, or specific text links.
- **Typography:** Minimalist Y2K style. Use crisp, geometric sans-serifs or monospace web fonts (e.g., 'Courier New', 'JetBrains Mono', 'Space Grotesk', or standard system-ui sans-serif tracked out heavily). Keep font weights light to medium, utilizing massive title scales juxtaposed with tiny, neat footnotes (similar to the layout in "image_580135.jpg").

### 2. GLOBAL NAVIGATION & HEADLESS MENU (Appears on every page)

- **Hover/Blur Interaction:** When a user hovers over this logo, it must trigger a site-wide background blur effect (`backdrop-filter: blur(10px)` applied to the main content container behind it).
- **Horizontal Dropdown Menu:** Simultaneously, upon hovering over the logo, a horizontal navigation dropdown menu must animate smoothly from left to right across the top of the screen. This menu links to all the major site sections (Home, CV, Projects, Creations).
